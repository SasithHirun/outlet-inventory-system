GEOTECHNICAL ADAPTABILITY

SOLUTIONS FOR DEMANDING GROUND CONDITIONS

Geology is never homogeneous. We customize fluid rheology to counteract the exact hydrostatic, frictional, and chemical forces of your strata.

Match Your Soil Type
HIGH RISK: CAVE-IN

Coarse Sand & Gravel

High permeability causes severe fluid loss and catastrophic sidewall collapse if the mud fails to bridge interstitial pore throats.

STAR Solution:

STAR-POLY™ + STAR-SEAL™ fibrous bridge. Forms an elastic membrane across permeable gravel layers.

SWELLING / BIT BALLING

Reactive Clays & Smectites

Water absorption causes rapid shale hydration, borehole narrowing, drill bit balling, and uncontrolled slurry viscosity spikes.

STAR Solution:

PHPA Polymer Clay Inhibitor. Encapsulates cuttings to prevent swelling and viscosity runaway.

TOTAL FLUID LOSS

Karst & Fissured Formations

Subterranean caverns and fissures drain hydrostatic head instantly, risking surface subsidence and stuck drilling tooling.

STAR Solution:

STAR-SEAL™ PLUG crosslinking pills. Expands 300x in cracks to lock in hydrostatic head within 5 minutes.

PRESSURE BLOWOUT

High Artesian Groundwater

Subsurface groundwater pressure exceeds the mud column head, diluting slurry and destabilizing foundation integrity.

STAR Solution:

STAR-BOND™ high-density bentonite systems weighted to 1.15-1.25 g/cm³ with Barite integration.

IONIC CONTAMINATION

Saline & Coastal Groundwater

High salinity flocculates conventional bentonite, causing sudden rheology collapse and failure to support trench walls.

STAR Solution:

Salt-Tolerant Polyglycol Polymers & Attapulgite clay matrices that remain unaffected by seawater ions.

ZERO-POLLUTION DISCHARGE

Urban & Eco-Sensitive Projects

Strict local environmental regulations forbid heavy bentonite sludge transport and disposal near public waterways.

STAR Solution:

100% Biodegradable STAR-POLY™ + STAR-BREAK™. Treated discharge passes direct sewer disposal norms.

INNOVATIVE SLURRY SOLUTIONS FOR DIFFICULT & UNFAVOURABLE DRILLING OPERATIONS

From massive urban diaphragm walls to mega-diameter bored piles and horizontal river crossings, we engineer fluids for extreme geotechnical loads.
