Annual Maintenance

Description

At Fire Contractors in Dubai, we provide comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) for all fire protection systems installed in residential, commercial, and industrial buildings across the UAE. Our AMC services ensure that your fire alarm systems, fire fighting equipment, emergency lighting, hydrants, fire pumps, and detection devices remain fully operational and compliant with Civil Defence regulations throughout the year. Regular maintenance improves system reliability, extends equipment life, and enhances safety for occupants and assets.

We conduct scheduled inspections, preventive servicing, testing, and corrective maintenance to detect and resolve issues before they escalate into faults or violations. Our certified technicians use industry-approved procedures and calibrated tools to perform thorough checks and documentation, helping you maintain compliance and safety standards at all times.

Inspection And Testing Of Fire Alarm Control Panels And Detection Devices
Servicing Of Fire Fighting Pump Sets And Associated Controls
Checks Of Hydrants, Hose Reels, Valves, And Extinguishers
Testing And Maintenance Of Emergency And Exit Lighting Systems
Documentation For Civil Defence Compliance And Certification

Fire safety is a critical responsibility. Partnering with an approved and experienced fire contractor means your systems are maintained with precision and care. Our AMC services provide peace of mind, reduce the risk of system failure, and support your regulatory requirements. Our team responds quickly to service requests and provides transparent reporting after each maintenance visit.
