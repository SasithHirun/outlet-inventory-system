Creating better spaces

From 2009 to today, Pintanna Estate in Ayagama, Ratnapura has evolved into Sri Lanka’s pioneering hub for sustainable Agarwood (Wallapatta) cultivation and luxury Oud creation.

Our Mission

To elevate Sri Lanka’s natural agarwood heritage to global recognition through ethical cultivation, organic inoculation, and artisanal hydro-distillation.

Our Vision

Guided by Mr. Kumar Dharmasena, we strive to ensure every drop of Pintanna Oud reflects the spirit of Sri Lanka—pure, refined, sustainable, and timeless.
